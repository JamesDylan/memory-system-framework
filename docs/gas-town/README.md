## What the source does not contain

The repo is a talk kit. It has no seat prompts, no scripts, no orchestrator config, no method for building the twin and no results for product work. The author says more detail will follow on LinkedIn.

## Limits of the method

- One small company's implementation, about a year old. Six people.
- Not shown at enterprise scale or under a commercial partnership.
- Yegge, whose design it builds on, saw his own version fail on a model upgrade and now argues for harnesses built into each application.
- The durable parts are the seat pattern, the laws and the ladder. Treat the tooling as replaceable.
